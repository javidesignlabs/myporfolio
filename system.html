05 · Rules

What the system enforces

  • Tokens firstEvery text, surface and border colour on the site resolves to a custom property. The light theme is those values swapped; no component has a light-mode rule of its own.
  • Headlines never break a wordDisplay type is sized per column so a long word fits from 375px up, instead of being hyphenated or clipped.
  • Motion is optionalReveals, the cursor and the screen reel all stand down under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Nothing tracks before consentFonts are self-hosted and analytics is injected only after an explicit accept.
  • No framework in the browserPlain HTML, one stylesheet and two small scripts, generated from a single content file.
